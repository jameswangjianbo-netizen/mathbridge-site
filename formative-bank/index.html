Paper 1 style — no calculator

Short structured questions, 5–7 marks, answers exact. Use one as a lesson starter, three as a unit formative, or eight as a Section A paper. Every answer is a surd, a fraction, a logarithm, an integer or an exact multiple of π — nothing here needs a calculator, and nothing here rewards one.

Paper 2 style — technology required

Applied contexts where the GDC is genuinely needed, not merely permitted. Answers to three significant figures, with the fuller value given in the markscheme so you can see whether a student rounded too early.

Paper 3 style — extended investigations

Eight complete Paper 3 questions of 22–28 marks, each developing one theme from concrete cases through a conjecture to a general result, and closing on a proof or an interpretation. They pair into four practice papers. Later parts restate any result they depend on, so a student who is stuck can still continue.

Quick checks — 1 to 3 marks

The last four minutes of a lesson, marked in one line. These replace the old exit tickets: same diagnostic purpose, but now written as marked questions so students practise earning R marks, which is where most reasoning marks are lost.

All questions are original, written in the style of the current HL papers and marked using the conventions of the published markschemes. Syllabus references follow the guide for first assessment 2021, updated November 2024. Companion to the AA HL Two-Year Course Map.
