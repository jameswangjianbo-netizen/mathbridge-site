The gap

Same subject, different language at every handoff

When a student moves from IGCSE into the MYP, or from MYP into DP Mathematics: AA, the mathematics is continuous — but the vocabulary, notation, and command terms often aren't. Teachers end up re-teaching material that was technically "covered" the year before, because nobody wrote down how the two syllabuses actually connect.

Without a bridge

  • Topics get re-taught from scratch, every handoff
  • Command terms shift without warning between programmes
  • HL/SL boundaries feel arbitrary instead of deliberate

With MathBridge

  • Each plan names the exact prior-programme skill it builds on
  • Command terms are mapped side-by-side across programmes
  • HL-only content is flagged clearly, never buried

FAQ

Before you ask

The questions we hear most from heads of department and classroom teachers.

Is the lesson plan pack really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no credit card. We ask for your email only so we can send the pack and follow up if you have questions.

What format are the lesson plans in?

Each plan is a print-ready PDF covering objectives, command terms, worked examples, and a vertical-articulation note, one page per topic per programme.

Do I need to be an IB World School to use these?

No. The IGCSE and MYP plans stand on their own, and the DP AA plans are useful whether or not your school runs the Diploma Programme.

Are these aligned to the current syllabus?

Yes. Every plan is cross-checked against the Cambridge IGCSE and Edexcel International GCSE specifications, the IB MYP framework, and the DP Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches guide, including the 2029 syllabus draft.

Will I be added to a mailing list?

No. We reply to your request directly and do not add you to any mailing list.

Yes. Once you have the pack it's yours to use and adapt across your team. Mention colleagues in the message field below and we'll loop them in directly.
