FAQ

Before you ask

The questions we hear most from marketing leads and founders.

Is the marketing audit really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to work with us afterward. We ask for your email so we can send the audit and follow up if you have questions.

How long is a typical engagement?

We start with a 90-day plan, then move month-to-month. There's no multi-year lock-in — you can leave at the end of any month.

What size businesses do you work with?

Mostly growing businesses that already have a product and some marketing spend in place. If you're pre-revenue or pre-launch, we're probably not the right fit yet.

Which industries do you work in?

Mainly B2B services, e-commerce, and multi-location local brands, but the audit itself is useful for almost any industry with an existing online presence.

How do you report on results?

A standing report on a fixed cadence, in plain language: what ran, what it cost, what it returned, and what changes next. No dashboard you have to learn to read yourself.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. After the initial 90-day plan, engagements run month-to-month.
